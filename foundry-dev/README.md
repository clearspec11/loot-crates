# Checking against a real Foundry world

`headless/run.cjs` joins a world as its Gamemaster in headless Chromium and runs a script as an async function body in the page, printing its return value as JSON. `headless/serve-module.py` serves this checkout's `module/` folder with CORS so the script can `import(MODULE_BASE + "scripts/x.js")` and test the working copy without installing anything on the Foundry host.

```
python3 foundry-dev/headless/serve-module.py module 8766 &
FOUNDRY_URL=http://192.168.0.57:30000 MODULE_BASE=http://<this machine>:8766/ \
  NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node foundry-dev/headless/run.cjs foundry-dev/checks/live-pull.js
```

Needs Playwright with Chromium 146 or newer (Foundry v14 refuses older) and a Gamemaster with no password: a dev world only. `checks/live-pull.js` does one real purchase on a test actor in the "Loot Crates Test" folder and reports the purse and the granted item. `checks/live-refund.js` does one pull at a fractional price, writes the log row, refunds it with `refundPull`, and reports that the purse is back to the same copper in whole, consolidated coins, the granted item is gone, the box's stock is restored, and a second refund changes nothing. `checks/live-refund-focus.js` does one pull, then refunds it from the manager's Pull log as a GM would (Refund, then Yes) and reports the purse and where keyboard focus lands (the row's "refunded <date>" status inside the log table). `checks/live-reveal.js` plays the reveal window with a strip built from the items compendium, writes nothing, and reports how far the landed card sits from the marker (`--shot` for a screenshot). `checks/live-missing.js` saves a test pool with one legendary entry pointing at a dead UUID, buys one crate through the GM's socket path, and reports the odds before and after (legendary gone), that the purse was charged once, and that the pool setting now flags the dead entry `missing: true`. It then opens the manager and reports the pool editor's rows: dead entries show "Source missing" (one the last sweep hadn't seen is flagged by the render), stale names show "was <old name>" with Update, the Boxes tab's odds leave the dead entries out, Update copies the source's name, and an item dropped on a missing row relinks it with its weight kept (`--shot` shows the pool). `checks/live-drop.js` makes sure an Item folder "Loot Crates Test" holds two test items, runs `entriesFromDrop` on the dnd5e.items compendium, that folder and a dnd5e.tables roll table, adds the first two to a test pool and reports the entries added; it then drops the folder on a second pool in the manager and clicks Add in the preview, drops the compendium and leaves its preview open (`--shot` shows it), and lists the packs the compendium picker offers. `checks/live-edits.js` makes no purchase: it opens the box editor, takes one from stock through the write queue while the dialog is open and saves a new price (stock stays lowered), opens a pool's rename dialog while a sweep flags its dead entry (the flag survives the rename), and relinks that entry with its Relink button and a typed UUID (weight kept). `checks/live-pool-users.js` makes no purchase: it saves a test pool with one item and a test box that draws only from it, reads the pool's header line ("In Live users check crate"), clicks the pool's Delete and then its item's Remove, reads each DialogV2 confirm for the box's name and "will have nothing inside", and clicks No both times (the pool and item must still be there).

`checks/live-edit-entry.js` makes no purchase: it saves a test pool (two uncommon dnd5e items, four weapons whose source has no rarity) and a box, and in the pool editor sets one entry to Legendary at weight 3 and another to weight 0 through the rows' own controls. It reports the stored entries, the row and header text ("Never drops", "No rarity set", "Set rarity for 4 unset"), the box odds before and after (Legendary in), 400 seeded draws (the weight-0 entry never drawn), and that a source sweep leaves the overridden entry ok, not renamed (`--shot` shows the pool).

`checks/live-sweep.js` makes no purchase: it registers the module's index fields as init does, warms dnd5e.items' index once, makes the call dnd5e's compendium browser makes (its own field list), then builds or reuses a pool of every dnd5e.items entry and times `checkSources` and `refreshMissing` twice each (every warm call must be under 1000 ms). It reports that the entries keep the tiers their index rows give and that the count flagged "No rarity set" matches the rows with no rarity, empties that pool again, puts a world Item with an empty `system.rarities` (created once in the Items folder "Loot Crates Test") into a small pool as an older entry and shows the editor flag it "No rarity set" after a sweep, and reports the "Live UI pool" entries learning their `sourceRarity` (`--shot` shows the editor).

`checks/live-big-pool.js` makes no purchase: it saves a pool of every dnd5e.items entry (872), opens the manager and times `render({force:true})` as render plus forced layout and as two animation frames less an idle two-frame baseline (both under 300 ms; the headless runner's software canvas makes two idle frames take ~500 ms). It reports that the big pool starts collapsed with its census in the summary and no rows, draws 50 rows when opened, that the "No rarity set" chip shows only unset rows, that a "wand" search plus one row set to Rare is stored by updatePool with focus kept, that the search and the Rare chip survive a full re-render, and that Show more draws rows 51-100 with focus on row 51. It empties the pool again at the end (`--shot` shows the open pool).

`checks/live-box-odds.js` writes nothing: it opens the box editor for the first box with a live pool, reads the preview pills, types a new weight with input events, unticks every pool (the "can drop nothing" line) and ticks them back, and reports the pills each time and that the stored box is unchanged; the dialog is left open in `THEME` (edit it to "light") for `--shot`.

`checks/live-focus.js` makes no purchase: it saves a pool of every dnd5e.items entry, types in its search and re-renders the manager before the search debounce runs (as a late source check does), then re-renders with focus on a row's rarity select. The typed text must survive with focus still in the search, and focus must come back to the same row's select. It empties its pool at the end.

`checks/live-shop-pity.js` saves a test pool and a 1 gp box "Live pity crate" (pity after 2, no duplicates), sets Crate Tester's pity state for it three ways and reads the shop card each time (both rarities with "within 2 pulls"; owns everything: "Nothing new for Crate Tester" on an aria-disabled Buy; due: only the guaranteed rarity, "guaranteed this pull"), checking the pills against `shopOdds` with that state. It picks Crate Tester in the shop's character select, then presses Buy once and watches every frame to the landing: the card's pity line and pills must stay as they were before the click from the GM's state write (~0.3 s into the spin) until this pull's card lands under the marker (`.lc-card--landed`), since the shop holds the result for the spin (0.7.0), and must then change to the new state within 1 s of the landing. It reports `heldAfterWriteMs`, `heldFrames` and `changedAfterLandingMs`, and that the reveal's ribbon was the odds the shop showed.

`checks/live-refund-pity.js` sets no state: it presses Buy once on "Live pity crate" in Crate Tester's shop (after live-shop-pity.js the pity is due, so the pull is the guaranteed rarity and resets the count), closes the reveal when it lands, opens the Pull log by clicking its tab, presses the new row's Refund and Yes, and reports Crate Tester's state for the box (must equal the state before the pull), the open shop card's pills and pity line (must equal the card before the pull, within 1 s of the state write), the dialog's pity and "can drop again" sentence, the purse and that the granted item is gone.

`checks/live-shop.js` makes no purchase and writes nothing: it opens the Crate Shop for Crate Tester and reports each box's pills, colour and Buy state, failing on any pill that reads 0% or has no rarity name, a box coloured above the best rarity it can drop, or an unbuyable (aria-disabled) Buy without an aria-describedby reason shown as visible text, or any Buy with a real `disabled` (it couldn't keep focus) (`--shot` shows the shop).

`checks/live-payoff.js` buys one crate as the GM through `requestPull` and reports, from the real reveal: the button reads Skip and is enabled mid-spin, the strip is aria-hidden, the result says "Added to <actor>'s inventory" and "<n> gp left", focus is on Close after landing, Open item renders the granted item's sheet, and the newest chat card's content link resolves via `fromUuid` to the granted item. It then opens two reveals with distinct requestIds (no purchase), reports their origins (cascaded, not stacked), closes the first mid-spin and confirms its chat card still posts.

Rules for the dev world: test actors only in that folder, never delete anything, never touch other folders' actors.

`headless/player-pull.cjs` times a real player purchase: a headless Gamemaster and Player2 both run the branch's socket code, and Player2 buys through the shop (`COUNT=3` buys three in a row; the first follows a fresh GM page load and its load-time sweep). It reports, per purchase, the click-to-reveal and click-to-landed times, the Buy button's labels over time, where keyboard focus is (`focus`: the Buy is focused before the click; after the GM's charge it must still be that same Buy node or the reveal's Close, Close on landing, and the Buy again once the reveal is closed; `focus.ok` sums it up), and the GM's per-step timings (`loot-crates | pull … timings (ms)`, a `console.debug` line socket.js always writes). Both clients also wire the branch's redraw listener as main.js does (open windows follow the world settings). `GM_LOG=1` opens the manager's Pull log on the headless GM first and reports `gmLog.rowMs`, from the click to the new row in the GM's `.lc-table` (within 2 s). `CLOSE_SHOP=1` buys nothing: the GM clicks the manager's shop toggle while the player's shop is open, and `closeShop` reports how long the player's Buy took to turn off with the notice naming the closed shop (within 2 s), then opens the shop again the same way. `SEAL=1` (with a `BOX` that has a pity rule, such as "Live pity crate") first opens the player's character sheet from the Actors sidebar and its Inventory tab, and `seal` reports the granted item's row: never visible in any animation frame before the landing (`leakFrames` 0), hidden at reveal +1 s with the shop card's pity text and odds as they were before the click, and visible at landed +0.5 s with the card showing the new state. With that sheet open the reveal reaches the player in ~2.4 s instead of ~0.5 s on this VM: dnd5e re-renders the sheet for the charge and again for the item, ~0.9 s each in software (`longTasks`, `sheetRenders`). Both clients' canvas ticker is paused unless `CANVAS=on`: headless Chromium draws the canvas in software, which on this VM makes every await on either page wait 0.5-1.4 s.

`headless/live-price.cjs` checks that a player is charged only the price on the Buy they pressed. Two headless clients run the branch's socket code (the Gamemaster seat must be free; it exits 2 when a person holds it). With Player2's shop open, the Gamemaster raises "Live UI crate"'s price in the manager as a person does (the Boxes tab, Edit, the price field, Save). Meanwhile the world's broadcast of that setting is held back on the player's WebSocket (Playwright `routeWebSocket`), so the shop still shows the old price. This is what happens to a pull already in flight when the GM saves. The player clicks that stale Buy. The check expects the warning toast ("… now costs N gp (you pressed M gp). Nothing was charged.") and the purse, items and Pull log unchanged. It then lets the broadcast through, waits for the Buy to redraw to the new price, and clicks again: the check expects exactly the new price to be charged, with a log row at that price. Finally the GM puts the old price back the same way. One real purchase per run.

`headless/live-well.cjs` checks the Wishing Well (well.js) at a real table, with the branch's code on both headless clients (the Gamemaster seat must be free). The GM seeds "Live well" (1 sp a throw from "Live UI pool", odds nothing 100, a cap of five throws more than Crate Tester has already thrown today, since the cap is per in-game day and the day doesn't turn between runs) and notes the world time, purse and log. Player2 presses the well's Throw five times in Crate Tester's shop, each reveal landing (`.lc-well--landed`) with "The coin sinks. Nothing." in the window's live region and no Watch again; the card counts "n of cap throws today" and after the fifth reads "The well is still until tomorrow (cap of cap today)" with its Throw off. No chat card may post; the purse must drop by exactly 50 cp and five rows carrying today's key (`day`) must be in the log. The GM then advances the world a day with dnd5e's calendar HUD (+1 day; the HUD is shown for the check if the world has it off and put back): the player's card must drop the still line within 2 s, and turning the day back brings it back. Then the GM opens the Pull log by its tab, presses Hide misses and Show misses, and Refund one then Yes on the folded "5 throws, nothing" row: the purse gains 1 sp, the row reads one throw fewer and the player's card "cap-1 of cap throws today" within 2 s. The world time, the calendar settings and the well's record are restored in a `finally`. `HIDE_GM=1` runs it with the GM's tab hidden.

`headless/live-dice.cjs` checks Dragon's Teeth (dicegame.js) at a real table, with the branch's code on every headless client (the Gamemaster seat must be free). The GM seeds "Live dice" (10 gp to roll 3d6 over the dragon's 3, so all but 1, 1, 1 win, paying 3x) and notes the purse, log and chat. Player2 presses Bet once in Crate Tester's shop; every animation frame to the landing (`.lc-dice--landed`) is watched for the verdict showing in the chat log (0 leaked frames) and each rendering of the pull's card must be sealed until then; the reveal's faces, total and result (in a live region, "15 beats the dragon's 3 · Rolled 4, 6, 5 on 3d6 · Won 30 gp · ... in the purse") are read, and the GM's reveal, and a third seat's that joined with its tab hidden (landing at once), must show the same faces. The newest chat message must carry no Roll, the purse must change by exactly payout - stake, and the log row must hold the faces and `payoutCp`. The GM then opens the Pull log by its tab, presses the row's Refund and Yes: the confirm reads "Take 20 gp back from Crate Tester?" (a win) or "Return 10 gp" (a loss), the purse returns to its start value, focus lands on the row's status and the card in chat reads refunded on the player's client. `HIDE_GM=1` runs it with the GM's tab hidden (its reveal lands at once). `MANUAL=1` sets the GM's d6 fulfillment to manual entry for the run (restored after): no manual-roll prompt may appear and the reveal must reach the player within 2.5 s. `SHORT=1` first spends the winnings (the purse set below what the refund would take back) and presses Refund: the dialog must name the shortfall ("Crate Tester has spent ... of the 30 gp won at Live dice ... Nothing was refunded") with no Yes, the row stays unrefunded and the purse and chat unchanged; the purse is then put back and the refund made. The dice configuration, the purse and the game's record are restored in a `finally`. One real purchase per run, refunded.

`headless/live-ownership.cjs` checks that the shop follows ownership while it is open, and makes no purchase. Two headless clients run the branch's shop and socket code (the Gamemaster seat must be free; it exits 2 when a person holds it). Player2's shop is open on Crate Tester. The Gamemaster takes Player2's ownership of Crate Tester down to Observer the way a GM does (the Actors sidebar, right-click, Configure Ownership, Player2's select, Save). Within 1 s of Save, the open shop must show no live Buy and must not name Crate Tester in the picker or the purse. The player then presses the crate's Buy anyway (a mouse click and Enter), and no `pull` may go out on the socket. There must also be no reveal and no toast. The GM then gives Owner back the same way. Crate Tester must come back in the same open window, with its Buy live, within 1 s. Player2's ownership is put back as it was in a `finally`, even when the check fails. Crate Tester stays in the "Loot Crates Test" folder.
